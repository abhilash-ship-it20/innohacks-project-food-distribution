From surplus to shared.

Three steps. One stronger community.

01

🥘 Donors share

Restaurants, stores and households post available food, servings, pickup location and a safe collection window.

02

📍 NGOs claim

Nearby volunteers and NGOs discover available listings, claim a donation and arrange a pickup time.

03

🌱 Impact grows

Track the handoff and count rescued food to make the community's impact visible.
